How it works

Seven steps from "I need a car" to a signed contract that matches the offer.

1

Your brief

How you will pay, your monthly or cash limit, the body style and the features you will not give up. Takes two minutes.

2

Your three

Three real models from Ourisman's lineup that fit the brief and the budget, with the reason each one made the list.

3

Your trade

Four photos, the odometer and the VIN. The desk sees the car before you arrive, so the allowance is in the written offer.

4

Payment and protection

Pick a lender rate to beat if you want one. Decide which protection products a dealer may quote, and which to skip.

5

Private address

Dealers write to a ClearDrive address you control. Your phone and email never leave the workspace.

6

Written offers

Each rooftop prices the exact car to your limit: selling price, fees, taxes, trade, payment. Ranked by terms. Nobody can buy a better spot.

7

The visit and the check

Book the drive. Enter the contract figures at the desk. Every line that differs from the offer is flagged with the question to ask.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really free for buyers?

Yes. Buyers never pay ClearDrive anything, for any feature. Dealers pay a flat fee per rooftop to receive requests. That fee does not buy ranking, visibility or access to the buyer's contact details.

What does a dealer actually see?

The model and trim, the payment type and limit, the required features, the trade with photos and odometer, the protection products you want quoted, and your private ClearDrive address. Not your phone number, email, home address or credit file.

How is this different from a car broker?

A broker negotiates for a fee, usually a few hundred dollars, and you are trusting one person's relationships. ClearDrive gives you written offers from every participating rooftop, ranks them by your own terms, and checks the contract against the offer. There is no fee and no middleman holding the information.

Can Ourisman see other dealers' offers?

No. Each desk sees the buyer's request and its own offer. It sees the lender rate the buyer chose, because that is the number it has to beat.

What happens if the contract does not match?

The paperwork check flags each line that differs and tells you what to ask. You decide whether to sign. After the sale, the three-question survey feeds the dealer's public grade.

Is this live today?

This is a demonstration built with sample data for the Ourisman pilot. No real customers, offers, dealer replies or CRM connections are active. The trust center lists exactly what is and is not connected.
