Privacy

What a dealer sees. What a dealer never sees.

The desk sees

  • The model, trim and body style you chose, and the two alternatives
  • How you will pay and your limit: monthly, due at signing or cash out the door
  • Your trade: year, make, model, mileage, condition, four photos and the loan payoff
  • The lender rate you chose, if any, because that is the number to beat
  • Which protection products you want quoted, declined or explained in person
  • Your private ClearDrive address and a first name

The desk never sees

  • Your phone number
  • Your personal email or home address
  • Your credit file, score or application
  • Any other dealer's offer
  • Your survey answers before the sale is complete
  • Anything after you close the private address

The private address is the only channel between a buyer and a rooftop. It forwards messages into the deal record and nowhere else. When the buyer closes it, every rooftop loses the ability to write.

Fairness rules

Six commitments, and the line of code behind each.

No dealer input affects ordering.
Offers are sorted by the buyer's own priority: lowest payment, lowest total cost, highest trade allowance or best fit. The sort function takes the buyer's priority and the priced offer. It has no field for subscription, fee or placement, so there is nothing a store could set.
A product you declined cannot carry a price.
Every quote passes through one guard, CD.cleanProd, before it is priced or shown. The guard keeps a price only for products marked "yes, quote it". Whatever a desk types for a declined product is dropped.
Both sides see the same number.
One function, CD.priceQuote, builds the out-the-door figure and the payment for the buyer's screen and the desk's screen from the same inputs: state tax rule, processing fee, title and registration, trade equity and the chosen rate.
Saved terms are frozen.
When a buyer books on an offer, that offer is copied into the deal record. A later revision creates a new offer beside it. Nothing overwrites the terms the buyer booked on, and the paperwork check compares the contract to the frozen copy.
Grades come from three questions after the sale.
Was the price the one you were shown? Did the paperwork match the offer? Was your time respected? Only buyers who completed a deal through ClearDrive are asked, and only those answers feed the public grade. No star ratings, no paid placement, no dealer replies to reviews.
Sample data is labeled.
Every demonstration screen carries "Demonstration · sample data" in the top bar, and every generated listing carries a sample marker. No screen presents a figure as a real customer, offer or dealer reply.

Security

Where security stands today, and what production adds.

Today this is a static demonstration. There are no accounts, no servers and no databases. Everything you enter stays in your own browser's storage on this device and is cleared with "Restart". No data leaves the page.

Production design

  • Separate buyer and dealer accounts with passkeys or verified email
  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest for every record
  • Least-privilege dealer roles: a desk sees only requests routed to its rooftop
  • An append-only audit log of every offer, revision and message
  • Trade photos stored with the deal record, never shared outside it
  • No sale, rental or exchange of buyer data, written into the terms

What we will not claim

No certification is claimed here. SOC 2 or similar attestation is part of the production plan and will be listed on this page only when an auditor has issued it. Until then, the honest statement is the one above: a demonstration with nothing to breach.

Your data

Kept for the deal. Deleted when you say so. Never resold.

Retention

A deal record is kept while the deal is open and for the ownership period the buyer chooses afterwards, so the paperwork check and the survey remain available. In this demonstration, the record lives only in your browser.

Deletion

A buyer can delete a deal record, including trade photos, from the record itself. In production, deletion is complete within 30 days, with the audit log keeping only that a deletion happened.

No resale

Buyer data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone other than the rooftops a buyer has chosen to ask. Lenders see only the rate request a buyer sends them. Dealers pay a flat fee; they do not buy data.

Accessibility

Usable by keyboard, legible by design.

  • Every control is reachable with Tab and operated with Enter or Space. Focus is visible as a teal ring.
  • Text contrast on the navy surfaces is at least 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large headings. Colors never carry a meaning alone; a label or icon accompanies them.
  • Motion respects the system "reduce motion" setting: the sky stops moving and entrance animations are removed.
  • Layouts hold from a 390-pixel phone to a wide desktop without horizontal scrolling. Tables scroll inside their own frame.
  • Images of vehicles carry the model name as alternative text. Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
